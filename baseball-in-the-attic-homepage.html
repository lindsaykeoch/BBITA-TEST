Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about our appraisal process.

An accurate appraisal protects you financially — whether you're insuring, selling, donating, or passing on your collection. Without a certified appraisal, you risk being underinsured, underpaid, or under-informed about the true value of your assets.
Most individual item appraisals are completed within 5–10 business days. Large collections or estate appraisals may take longer depending on the scope. Michael will provide a timeline estimate during your initial consultation.
Valuations are based on recent comparable auction results, dealer sales records, market trends, condition assessment, authentication status, provenance, and Michael's 30+ years of specialized expertise.
An independent appraiser like Michael has no financial stake in your collection. Dealers and auctioneers profit from buying low and selling high — creating an inherent conflict of interest. Michael's only goal is to provide you with an accurate, unbiased valuation.
Consider your purpose: insurance coverage, estate planning, potential sale, or simple curiosity. Gather any documentation, receipts, or provenance information you have. Take clear photographs if submitting remotely. Michael will guide you through everything during the initial consultation.
